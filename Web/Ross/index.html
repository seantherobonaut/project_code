QUIZZES

The quizzes are online multiple choice.   You can do any quiz at any time during the semester, BUT all quizzes must be complete before 11:59pm Thursday 19 December.   I suggest you spread it out and take each quiz as soon as you have completed the corresponding material.   You can retake each quiz as many times as you like, BUT each time you retake a quiz you will be penalized 10%.   That is, for each 10 point quiz, the recorded score, Q, is given by:

 

Q = highest score + 1 - count

 

Example 1 , assume you took "Quiz X" 1 time with a score of 10.   So, your highest score was 10, and your count was 1.   Thus, your Recorded Score will be    10 + 1 – 1 = 10.

 

Example 2 , assume you took "Quiz X" 3 times with scores 7, 9, 6.   So, your highest score was 9, and your count was 3.   Thus, your Recorded Score will be    9 + 1 – 3 = 7.
